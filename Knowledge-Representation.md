## Knowledge Graphs (KG)

A **Knowledge Graph (KG)** is a *structured representation of knowledge* where **entities** (such as people, places, products, diseases) are represented as **nodes**, and their **relationships** are represented as **edges**.

- Nodes
- Edged
- Properties

A knowledge Graph enables structured data, semantic search, and even reasoning capabilities. They are commonly used in search engines, medical knowledge, and social relationships.

### LLMs integration with KGs

Knowledge graphs can help LLMs reason, typically LLMs use pattern recognition for reasoning tasks but when paired with a KG they can use deductive reasoning by following the relationship paths within the KG. 

This follows for fighting hallucinations as well and allows us to incorporate new data more dynamically, it also combats the problem of depth in LLMs, by allowing the LLM to follow the chain of relationships.

### Web Ontology Language (OWL)

OWL is a semantic web language used to represent complex knowledge and meta data that can be easily understood by a computer. OWL Ontologies can be viewed by a free and open source software called Protege
